## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Added `toArabicNumbers()` extension on `String` to convert English digits to Eastern Arabic numerals.
- Added `toCurrency()` extension on `num` for multi-locale currency formatting using the `intl` package.
- Added `isValidEgyptianPhone()`, `isEgyptianPhone` getter, and `formatEgyptianPhone()` extensions on `String` for Egyptian mobile number validation and formatting.
